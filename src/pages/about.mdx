# About

I make complex systems comprehensible. I've spent **7+ years** writing and
designing content for data-oriented enterprise software — at **Ataccama**
(data quality and data management) and **Oracle NetSuite** (ERP) — for
audiences that range from engineers and data practitioners to non-technical
stakeholders.

What I enjoy most is owning documentation **end-to-end**: from deploying the
infrastructure that reviewers use, down to the microcopy on a single button.

## What I do

- **Documentation** — docs-as-code, AsciiDoc, Antora, Markdown, XML, VS Code,
  OpenAPI, Algolia.
- **Content design & writing** — conversation flows, UX writing, voice &
  tone, prompt engineering, agentic UX.
- **Infrastructure & version control** — GitHub, GitLab, AWS.
- **AI tools & prototyping** — Claude / Claude Code, custom GPTs, GitHub
  Copilot, OpenCode; applied to docs workflows in practical, auditable,
  repeatable ways.

## Core competencies

- **Systems thinking** — driving documentation and content design projects
  end-to-end, from technical deployment to individual articles and microcopy.
- **AI as multiplier** — accelerating docs workflows with AI in ways that are
  practical and repeatable, including contributor-facing tooling.
- **Cross-audience communication** — translating complex technical systems
  for engineers, data practitioners, and non-technical users alike.
